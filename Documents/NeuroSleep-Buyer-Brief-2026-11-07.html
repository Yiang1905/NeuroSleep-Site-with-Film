YIANG / NEUROSLEEP · CONFIDENTIAL PRELIMINARY DISCUSSION

NeuroSleep

Concept-stage sleep-support project · Strategic fit and outright asset-transfer enquiry
Overview. NeuroSleep explores a future combination of sleep-position sensing, adjustable neck support and a connected digital experience. It is presented for strategic evaluation by an established sleep, bedding, consumer-hardware or connected-product company.

What exists today

Digital presentation

Project website, brand direction, concept narrative and visual materials.

Concept film

Visual storytelling of the intended experience; not functioning hardware.

Concept documents

Product positioning, application hypotheses and preliminary architecture direction.

Browser demonstrator

Transparent rule-based simulation; not trained AI or validated control software.

Potential strategic relevance

Potentially relevant capabilities include smart pillows, mattresses, adjustable support mechanisms, sleep sensing, connected-home products, industrial design, embedded systems, apps and consumer-hardware manufacturing.

Home, hospitality, student living, mobility, travel, relaxation seating and healthcare-related research are application hypotheses only. No market demand or technical feasibility has been validated.

Current stage — important limitations

Concept stage only. No independently verified integrated hardware prototype, filed/granted patent, clinical validation, medical-device approval, validated proprietary AI model, demonstrated health benefit, revenue, paying customers, confirmed production supply chain or dedicated R&D team is claimed.

Sensing, actuation, algorithms, app and cloud functions remain proposed directions requiring independent engineering, safety, privacy, regulatory and commercial assessment. This is not a finished or market-ready medical product.

Prepared 7 November 2026 · Preliminary discussion only · All technical and commercial claims remain subject to independent assessment.
