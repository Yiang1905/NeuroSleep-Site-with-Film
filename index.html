ONE CORE PLATFORM · SEVEN APPLICATION PATHS

Designed Around Where People Sleep

NeuroSleep is exploring one adaptable sleep-support platform with different potential configurations for hospitality, home, student accommodation, automotive seating, portable travel support, massage seating and healthcare environments. These are target-use hypotheses—not launched products or validated demand.

One Core. Different Environments.

The proposed platform brings together validated inputs, interpretable decision logic, user control and safety constraints. Materials, feature sets, service workflows and commercial models could then be adapted to each environment after feasibility and user research.

Sense & validate

Potential inputs must be technically feasible and checked for quality.

02

Any future adaptation would need bounded responses, clear explanations and safety review.

03
Keep people in control

Manual override, privacy and safe failure behavior are core design requirements.
