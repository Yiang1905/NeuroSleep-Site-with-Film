02 / Application map

Seven paths to investigate.
None validated yet.

The pathways are a prioritisation map for buyer discussion, not a claim that one core platform is already engineered or commercially proven.

01 / HOSPITALITY

Guest rooms and hotels

Potential questions: can a configuration be durable, easy to clean, simple for guests to control and manageable for housekeeping? Validate service workflow, maintenance cost and hotel willingness to pilot.

Potential partner types: hotel groups, bedding suppliers, guest-room technology providers.
02 / HOME

Personal sleep support

Potential questions: is the benefit understandable, comfort acceptable, setup simple and price realistic? Test ergonomics, safety, reliability and willingness to pay.

Potential partner types: smart-bedding brands, sleep-tech companies, consumer hardware makers.
03 / STUDENT LIVING

Dormitories and shared accommodation

Potential questions: can a lower-cost configuration withstand shared use, support simple servicing and avoid unnecessary personal-data collection?

Potential partner types: student housing operators, dormitory suppliers, education procurement teams.
04 / MOBILITY

Vehicle seating

Potential questions: could support be explored without interfering with seat belts, airbags, driving safety or vehicle controls? Specialist automotive engineering and safety review would be essential.

Potential partner types: seat suppliers, mobility technology firms, vehicle manufacturers.
05 / TRAVEL

Portable neck support

Potential questions: can a concept meet weight, packability, washable-material, fit and price constraints? Electronics may be inappropriate if they compromise simplicity or comfort.

Potential partner types: travel accessory brands, luggage companies, consumer product manufacturers.
06 / RELAXATION

Massage and relaxation seating

Potential questions: can a support mechanism integrate safely with existing chair programs, bounded movement, clear stop controls and safe failure behavior?

Potential partner types: massage chair makers, wellness equipment brands, seating OEMs.
07 / HEALTHCARE-RELATED

Care environments

This is a high-barrier research direction only. Clinical workflow, medical-device classification, regulatory obligations, privacy, safety and specialist review would be needed before any healthcare product claim.

Potential partner types: qualified medical-device or care-equipment companies, subject to regulatory review.
Strategic hypotheses, not market evidence. No customer interviews, paid demand, product-market fit, clinical benefit or manufacturing feasibility is claimed here. A buyer should choose one pathway and validate it before investing in a broader platform.
