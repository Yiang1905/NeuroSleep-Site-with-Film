01 / Product concept

Four ideas. One unproven system hypothesis.

NeuroSleep explores a sleep-support system that could connect posture-related inputs, adjustable neck support, bounded decision logic and a digital companion. This is a direction for evaluation—not a demonstrated product.

System direction

Adapt · Monitor · Protect · Connect

01

Adapt

Explore adjustable neck support and comfort preferences. Mechanism, noise, force limits, durability and comfort need engineering design and tests.

02

Monitor

Potential inputs might include pressure or posture-related signals. No physical sensors or data pipeline are currently integrated.

03

Protect

Signal validation, bounded response, manual override and safe failure are requirements to investigate, not verified safety capabilities.

04

Connect

A future digital experience might present settings or user-entered information. No working app, health inference or clinical capability is claimed.
